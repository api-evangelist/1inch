## Aqua Learn (guides, FAQ, and key concepts)
- [Learn 1inch Aqua](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn): Hub for Aqua guides, key concepts, video tutorials, and FAQ.
- [How Aqua works](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/how-aqua-works): Shared liquidity explained — one wallet balance backs many positions, tokens move only when a swap fills.
- [Why Aqua](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/why-aqua): The properties that define Aqua — shared liquidity, JIT protection, verified counterparties, self-custody.
- [Shared liquidity](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/why-aqua/shared-liquidity): Why pooled liquidity sits idle and how Aqua raises asset utilization.
- [JIT protection](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/why-aqua/jit-protection): What JIT liquidity sniping is and why Aqua positions are protected by design.
- [Aqua vs AMM and RFQ](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/why-aqua/vs-amm-rfq): How Aqua compares to pooled AMMs and RFQ systems.
- [Aqua vs staking and restaking](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/why-aqua/vs-staking-restaking): Same reuse idea, different rules — nothing deposited, no slashing, no exit queues.
- [The replay](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/why-aqua/backtests): The same deposit replayed for the last full year on public Dune data — a replay, not a backtest: the Uniswap leg is measured history, the Aqua leg a stated routing model — under two competition states (sole strategy / fully contested) and finite wallet balances walked daily against the measured signed flow — opens with the LP census of the top-10 Uniswap v3 pools (32.6% of all 348,993 positions are $10K or less and collected 0.8% of the fees), then the shared-wallet capability (a one-pair-to-six positions ladder: 3.3x from the same $10K in either competition state), a Uniswap-pool-vs-Aqua-strategy head-to-head on the same pair and price range ($456–$464 pool vs $42–$410 sole strategy vs $205 fully contested at $10K) with a bridge to JIT protection (the measured JIT share subtracted from the pool side has no Aqua equivalent), a fee sweep with a break-even window (1.2 to 4.82 bps on ETH/USDC at $10K, sole-strategy upper end; no fee beats the pool once competing strategies match its book) flanked by the pair's own tier boundaries (0.01% and 0.05%), six isolated pool deposits vs one shared four-token wallet (2.7x the fees at the upper end as the sole strategy; fully contested it stays ahead on the all-flow end, 1.3x at $683 vs $509, and falls behind on the aggregator-routed floor, $87 vs $482) with the per-pair breakdown at both $10K and $1B (at the top end five of six pairs still favor the shared wallet, up to 1.6x on ETH/USDC, while the smallest pool flips to the split), both shapes scaled across sixteen budgets from $10K to $1B with the measured book marked on the budget-axis charts ($204.6M across the six pools; $80.9M for the ETH/USDC head-to-head — rungs past the line illustrate saturation, not fee estimates), and a limits list carrying the slow-quote disclosure (loss-versus-rebalancing / intraday adverse selection is not charged on the Aqua side, which flatters it — read Aqua figures toward the lower ends).
- [Aqua for institutions](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/why-aqua/institutions): A verified counterparty environment with full self-custody for funds and treasuries — provide liquidity without handing over keys or tokens.
- [Aqua security](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/security): Audits, the revocable allowance model, and risk boundaries.
- [Liquidity provision risks](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/risks): The risks of providing liquidity compared across AMM pools, RFQ systems, and Aqua — custody, smart-contract risk, impermanent loss, stale-price pickoff.
- [Sub-wallets](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/sub-wallets): Gnosis Safe accounts controlled by your main wallet, with isolated balances, one-signature batches and multisig support.
- [Create your first position](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/create-first-position): Step-by-step guide to opening a liquidity position.
- [Strategy types](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/strategy-types): Straight vs Curved positions and when to use each.
- [Concentrated (Straight)](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/strategy-types/concentrated): Straight positions that quote inside a price range you choose — how ranges behave, what happens at the edges and how to think about width.
- [Full range (XYC)](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/strategy-types/xyc): The classic constant-product curve behind the Full range tier — liquidity spread across every price, so the position is never out of range.
- [Pegged (Curved)](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/strategy-types/pegged): Curved positions for pairs that should hold at or near parity, like stablecoin pairs or ETH liquid-staking tokens.
- [Fees, earnings and APR](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/fees-earnings-apr): Where Aqua earnings come from and how APR is displayed.
- [Manage and close positions](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/manage-close): How to monitor, manage and close liquidity positions.
- [Aave collateral loops](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/aave-collateral-loops): Using Aave aTokens as Aqua position backing — how leverage loops work, a real July 2026 position replayed through its liquidation, health-factor and price-buffer math, and an interactive loop planner.
- [Aqua FAQ](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/faq): Answers on custody, earnings, and access.
- [Build on Aqua](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/developers): Aqua SDK, SwapVM SDK, and developer documentation links.
- [Aqua for resolvers](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/resolvers): Resolver onboarding — verification, canonical contract addresses, discovering positions with the Aqua API or on-chain events, and a protected quote-and-fill quickstart with the SwapVM SDK.
- [Video tutorials](https://1inch.com/aqua/learn/tutorials): Short video walkthroughs of every Aqua flow.
