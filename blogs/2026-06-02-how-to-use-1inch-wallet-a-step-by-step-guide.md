This guide introduces 1inch Wallet, a self-custodial mobile crypto wallet that allows users to store digital assets, swap tokens, and interact with DeFi applications from one place. The article walks through seven essential steps: creating or importing a wallet, understanding the interface, funding the account, sending tokens, executing swaps with MEV protection, exploring Web3 apps, and implementing security best practices like safeguarding recovery phrases and avoiding suspicious dApps.
